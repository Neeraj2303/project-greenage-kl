1. PROJECT BACKGROUND & OBJECTIVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The GREENAGE-KL study is designed to address a critical healthcare challenge: the
cardiovascular crisis in Kerala, where Coronary Artery Disease (CAD) prevalence 
substantially exceeds national averages and manifests early, disproportionately 
afflicting the 20-40 age group. 

Objectives:
*   Early Molecular Detection: Utilizes Fatty Acid Binding Protein (FABP) 
    quantified by near-infrared (NIR) spectroscopy as a novel pre-clinical biomarker.
*   Precision Nutrition Modulation: Evaluates the efficacy of sulforaphane-rich 
    cruciferous microgreens (broccoli, red radish, fenugreek; 20-50 g/day) as 
    epigenetic modulators targeting DNA methylation, histone acetylation, and 
    reducing NRF2/KEAP1-axis oxidative stress.
*   Chaos-to-Order Genomics: Integrates a dual-layer Shannon Codon Entropy (J')
    framework to calculate codon degeneracy scores and model biological resilience 
    against genomic vulnerability.
*   Cohort Study Design: A 12-month prospective cohort enrolling 500 participants 
    (250 in the TEST group, and 250 age-sex matched CTRLs) across all 14 Kerala 
    districts.

2. SOFTWARE ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The system is implemented as a lightweight, high-performance Single-Page 
Application (SPA) consisting of three core static web modules:

*   index.html: Defines the structure, layout, top progress banners, enrollment 
    mini-map, and 16 modular form panels.
*   styles.css: Implements visual tokens, clean grids, responsive tables, flex 
    sidebar layouts, and modern visual states (active, completed, warning, error).
*   script.js: Coordinates patient selection, local caching, CSV exports, dynamic 
    event listeners, and automated clinical calculations.

Data is stored client-side in browser LocalStorage under the key "greenage_data" 
as an aggregated JSON cohort database. On load, the file reads the data cache 
and populates the visual enrollment progress mini-map dynamically.

3. STATE FLOW & PATIENT COHORT NAVIGATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Records are retrieved and updated using a combined Unique Record Key:
Record Key Format: "Group-PID-Timepoint" (e.g., "TEST-005-BL" or "CTRL-102-M3")
*   Group: "TEST" (Test Group) or "CTRL" (Control Group).
*   Participant ID (PID): Range from 1 to 250.
*   Timepoint: Baseline ("BL"), Month 3 ("M3"), Month 6 ("M6"), or Month 12 ("M12").

Enrollment Mini-Map:
Generates a 250-cell visual grid matching the active group (squares for Test, 
circles for Control). Cells are color-coded to show status:
*   Green (Teal): Saved (Data exists for Baseline).
*   Amber: Current active patient.
*   Gray: Pending enrollment (No data saved).

4. MODULAR DATA PANELS & CLINICAL CALCULATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The application is partitioned into 16 sections, each with dedicated logic:

A. Demographics & Identification (Panel: p_demo)
   - Inputs: Name, Study Code, Date of Birth, District (14 Kerala districts), 
     Sex, Domicile, Consent Date, Lifestyle variables, etc.
   - Calculations: 
     * Age = Math.floor((Current Date - Date of Birth) / (365.25 * 24 * 3600 * 1000))

B. Anthropometry (Panel: p_anthro)
   - Inputs: Height (cm), Weight (kg), Waist, Hip, Neck, Body Fat %, Muscle Mass.
   - Calculations:
     * BMI = Weight / (Height / 100)^2
     * BMI Category (Asian Specific): Underweight (<18.5), Normal (18.5 to 22.9), 
       Overweight (23 to 24.9), Obese I (25 to 29.9), Obese II (>=30)
     * Waist-Hip Ratio (WHR) = Waist / Hip

C. Blood Pressure & Vital Signs (Panel: p_bp)
   - Inputs: Systolic/Diastolic readings (s1, s2, d1, d2), Heart Rate, SpO2, Temp.
   - Calculations:
     * Mean SBP = (s1 + s2) / 2
     * Mean DBP = (d1 + d2) / 2
     * Pulse Pressure = SBP Average - DBP Average
     * Mean Arterial Pressure (MAP) = DBP Average + (Pulse Pressure / 3)
     * AHA BP Classification: Normal, Elevated, Stage 1 HTN, Stage 2 HTN.

D. Laboratory Tests (Panel: p_labs)
   - Inputs: Lipids (TC, LDL, HDL, TG), Glycemia (FBG, Insulin, HbA1c), Renal 
     and Hepatic markers (Creatinine, eGFR, ALT, AST, hsCRP, NT-proBNP, Vit D).
   - Calculations:
     * Non-HDL-C = Total Cholesterol - HDL
     * TC/HDL Ratio = Total Cholesterol / HDL
     * LDL/HDL Ratio = LDL / HDL
     * VLDL = Triglycerides / 5
     * HOMA-IR = (FBG * Insulin) / 405

E. FABP-NIR Spectroscopy (Panel: p_fabp)
   - Inputs: Spectroscopy Result (ng/mL), Wavelengths, Batch No.
   - Calculations:
     * FABP Category: Normal (<2), Mildly Elevated (2-6), Elevated (6-10), 
       High Risk (>=10 ng/mL).

F. Echocardiography (Panel: p_echo)
   - Inputs: LVEF, GLS, E-wave, A-wave, e' Septal, e' Lateral.
   - Calculations:
     * LVEF Category: Normal (>=55), Mildly Reduced (40-54), Reduced (<40).
     * GLS Category: Normal (<= -20%), Mildly Abnormal (-18% to -20%), 
       Subclinical LV Dysfunction (> -18%).
     * E/A Ratio = E-wave / A-wave
     * E/e' Ratio = E-wave / ((e' Septal + e' Lateral) / 2)

G. CardioSense HRV (Panel: p_hrv)
   - Inputs: SDNN, RMSSD, pNN50, LF/HF, Shannon Entropy.
   - Calculations:
     * Autonomic Balance: Parasympathetic Dominance (<1.0), Balanced (1.0 to 2.5), 
       Mild Sympathetic Dominance (2.5 to 4.0), Sympathetic Dominance (>4.0).

H. Oxidative Stress (Panel: p_oxid)
   - Inputs: MDA, SOD, GPx, Catalase, GSH, GSSG, 8-OHdG.
   - Calculations:
     * GSH/GSSG Ratio = GSH / GSSG
     * Oxidative Stress Index = (MDA / SOD) * 100
     * MDA/SOD Categories: Normal vs. Elevated Oxidative Stress.

I. Epigenetic Markers (Panel: p_epigen)
   - Inputs: Global 5-mC, NRF2 Promoter Methylation, DNMT Levels, qPCR expressions.

J. SNP Genotyping Panel (Panel: p_snp)
   - Inputs: Genotypes for ACE, PCSK9, HMGCR, MTHFR, TCF7L2, FTO, PPARG, NRF2.
   - Calculations:
     * Genotype-specific risk allele mapping, statin response, and Folate actions.
     * Genomic Risk Score (0-16 points scale).
     * Risk Tertile classification (Low: 0-5, Intermediate: 6-10, High: 11-16).

K. Shannon J' Entropy Framework (Panel: p_shannon)
   - Inputs: Observed Shannon H' value across 21 codon degeneracy families.
   - Calculations:
     * Family Resilience J' = H' / H_max
     * Composite Mean J' = Average J' of all active amino acid families.
     * Intervention Trigger: Escalate sulforaphane dose if >=3 low J' families.

L. Cardiovascular Risk Scores (Panel: p_scores)
   - Inputs/Outputs: AHA PREVENT score and Framingham (FRS) scores.

M. Microgreen Intervention Compliance (Panel: p_micro)
   - Inputs: Fortnightly consumed/delivered logs across 26 fortnights.
   - Calculations:
     * Compliance % = (Consumed Dose / Delivered Dose) * 100
     * Overall Cohort Compliance % = Average of all completed fortnights.

N. Adverse Events & Safety (Panel: p_adverse)
   - Inputs: AE severity log, relationship to intervention, SAE classification.

O. Participant Data Summary (Panel: p_summary)
   - Calculations: Dynamic validation of completed inputs per participant, 
     displaying Complete/Partial/Pending badges and lists of missing fields.
